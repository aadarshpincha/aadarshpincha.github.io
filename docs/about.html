About

Finance professional.
Indian equities analyst.

I'm Aadarsh — a finance professional based in Bangalore with a focus on Indian listed equities. My work sits at the intersection of fundamental research, multi-asset analysis, and investment idea generation.

My approach to investing is grounded in business fundamentals — I look for companies with durable competitive advantages, high-quality earnings, and management teams that think like owners. Growth is a necessary condition, but I won't overpay for it.

I passed CFA Level I in February 2025 with a score of 1770/1900, placing in the top 10% globally. I'm currently a CFA Level II Candidate, deepening my understanding of equity valuation, fixed income, and derivatives.

Before focusing on equity research, I completed a B.Sc. in Economics from St. Joseph's College of Commerce, Bangalore, graduating with an 8.62 GPA. I was a rank holder in Mathematics at ISC Grade 12, scoring 97.4%.
